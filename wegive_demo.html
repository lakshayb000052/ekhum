Stage 3

Approaches for the fundraiser to choose from

Awaiting selection

Three ways to spend the same ₹1,50,000

The tool does not pick. It builds three costed approaches — channel mix, bid strategy, creative count — and the fundraiser approves exactly one. The two rejected approaches stay on the record with the reason.

APPROACH C
Lapsed-donor lift

Cheapest per rupee raised. WhatsApp plus display retargeting against donors who gave once and stopped.

Bid strategyMax conv · no tCPA

Assumptions carried into every number above. Average gift ₹1,800 and 0.9% display CTR are unsourced placeholders in this demo. Before any of this reaches ChildFund, each input needs a named source and a validation note — modelled projections presented as measured results are a diligence liability.
